# The Pipcast videos

The demo video, and KLWD WEATHER's promo, made with [Remotion](https://www.remotion.dev). Everything KLWD shows in it is the product's own: the design system's components from `design/components`, the band's plate from `packages/mod`, the words from `packages/contract`, the jingles and the beat from `plugin/station/sounds` and the icon's layers from `assets/icon-layers`. Nothing here changes them.

```bash
npm ci
```

```bash
npm run studio
```

opens Remotion Studio. `npm run render` renders the video to `out/pipcast.mp4`, and `npm run render:4k` at twice the size, for YouTube. Render the 4K one with `npm run render:4k -- --concurrency=1 --timeout=120000`: at a concurrency of 3 or 5 it left broken frames in the video, such as a half-white screen, a blank window or a stalled crawl, even on an idle Mac. `out/` is not committed.

## How it is put together

- `src/timeline.ts` is the running order: when each scene starts, where each jingle and the beat play, and the soundtrack's plan. The picture and the music both read it, so a cut moved there moves the music with it.
- `src/program.ts` and `src/stories.ts` are what KLWD airs at each second: the board, each story's BREAKING NEWS, desk and outro, and the report on the hour.
- `src/design` plays the design system's components frame by frame. The opens and outros take their progress, the crawl its offset, Clawd his pose, and the CSS motion a custom property per frame (`motion.ts`), so every frame is the same picture however it is rendered. For the same reason the window's glass controls are drawn without `backdrop-filter`: headless Chrome leaves a backdrop-filtered layer out of some of its frames, and the controls blinked.
- `src/desktop` is the Mac: the wallpaper, the menu bar, the Claude desktop app, a browser, the code editor on the second desktop and the pointer.
- `src/stage` is the screen at one moment (`Screen.tsx`) and the fixed frame it sits in on the paper (`Frame.tsx`). The camera only zooms inside that frame, never wider than the whole screen and never past its edges, and it holds perfectly still between moves. The scenes keep it on the whole screen and lean in only to read something: a second to get there, under three seconds there, a second back.
- `src/look/HeadlineBand.tsx` is the line above the frame that says what is happening, one line at a time.
- `src/scenes` are the scenes: the cold open, the name card, where the second capital P of PiP drops out as a lowercase p comes down into its place, the desktop session from tuning in to the window's tricks (`Desk.tsx`, one script for the camera, the pointer, the windows and KLWD: another desktop sliding in while the window stays put, a new corner, the side, a short story that brings the window out of the side and sends it back, its tab, a smaller size), and the finale, where the frame becomes the icon's tile and the icon builds itself, its signal going out of the beacon a ring at a time on the music's notes.
- `src/Soundtrack.tsx` lays the soundtrack, the jingles, the beat and the sound effects on the picture, at levels measured as a mix: about -16 LUFS, with the true peak under -1 dBFS.
- `scripts/music/score.js` is the soundtrack's score, in D major around the station's four-note logo, with the jingles' own instruments. It follows the plan in `src/timeline.ts` and never stops: only while KLWD's own sound plays does it break down, to a held D under BREAKING NEWS and its beat and to a soft D major pad under the hour's fanfare and its beat, and as soon as the beat goes, the music's own beat comes back over the D until the groove returns. `scripts/render-audio.mjs` renders it and the sound effects in Chrome, as `scripts/render-jingles.mjs` renders the jingles.
- `scripts/render-beat.mjs` lays KLWD's beat where the station plays it: the shipped loops from `plugin/station/sounds`, from each desk to its outro, mixed in and phased out, or hushed when a story is cut, with the contract's own lengths.
- `scripts/prepare.mjs` runs before the studio and every render: it copies the design system's stylesheet without its Google Fonts import, copies the icon's layers as markup, renders the soundtrack in Chrome when its score changed, and lays the beat when its loops or where it plays changed. What it makes is in `src/generated` and `public/audio`, which are not committed.
- `scripts/stills.mjs <composition> <dir> <seconds...>` renders single frames, for checking a change without rendering the video.
- `src/readme` lays out the README's three grids of screenshots from the same pieces, as the video's boards are laid out: moments of the desktop scene, two states the video only passes through (the window's controls on hover, and the transparent window), and KLWD's pictures in its window. `npm run readme` renders them into `assets/screenshots` at the repository's root. It runs `scripts/prepare.mjs --picture` first, which stops before the soundtrack and the beat, since a still has no sound.
- `src/thumbnail` is the video's YouTube thumbnail: the icon, the name in the serif and the line under it, centred on the paper. `npm run thumbnail` renders it into `assets/youtube-thumbnail.jpg` at the repository's root, after `scripts/prepare.mjs --picture` as for the README's screenshots. It also renders `assets/youtube-thumbnail-play.jpg`, the same picture as YouTube's embedded player shows it before it plays, which the README shows at its top as its link to the video. `YouTubeOverlay.tsx` draws the player over it: the gradient and title bar, the play button, and the share and Watch on YouTube buttons, as a visitor who isn't signed in sees them, with the player's own sizes, colours, fonts and icons, read from youtube.com/embed at the width the README shows the picture at. Its title, its channel and `channel-avatar.png`, the channel's picture, are the video's on YouTube.

## KLWD WEATHER's promo

`KlwdWeather` in the Studio is a promo for KLWD WEATHER, made as a 90s station's severe-weather promo from the same pieces. `npm run render:weather` renders it to `out/klwd-weather.mp4`, and `npm run render:weather:4k` at twice the size.

- `src/promo/timeline.ts` is its running order, one shot of the storm from start to end: the storm over KLWD's tower until lightning strikes the beacon and sends out its signal, the question and the show's name in chrome over the storm, then KLWD's window flying out of the beacon to show the forecast a part at a time (`FEATURES`), each named beside it with when it airs and what it does, flipping from one to the next, STORM WARNING breaking in on it and the camera leaning into STORMTRACKER, and the window flying home before the storm clears into the icon's dusk as the camera pulls back to the icon and its name. The picture and the sound both read it.
- `src/promo/Promo.tsx` is the shot: one camera for the whole of it, the window's flight, flips and lean, and the captions beside it.
- `src/promo/World.tsx` is the icon's city at night, built from `assets/icon-layers` and seen through one camera in the icon's own units, with the lightning, the beacon and its signal. `src/promo/StormSky.tsx` draws its sky on the GPU from the time alone: the storm clouds rolling in from the left, lit by the lightning and the city, the rain on the lens, and the icon's dusk they clear to.
- `src/promo/Cards.tsx` slams the words in with the stinger's chrome lettering, as WEATHER ALERT's do.
- `src/design/StormTracker.tsx` draws STORMTRACKER at a moment of its 13 seconds: the design system's globe runs itself on the browser's clock, so the video keeps one scene and hands it the time. `OnAir.tsx` draws KLWD WEATHER's pages, WEATHER ALERT and its outro, holding the open's stylesheet motion paused at the scene's time.
- `scripts/music/storm.js` is its sound: the rain, the thunder of each flash, and a score in D minor that ends in D major as the storm clears, played through `score.js`'s synth and mix. `scripts/render-audio.mjs storm` renders it into `public/audio/storm.wav`, and `scripts/render-beat.mjs promo` lays KLWD's beat where the promo plays it into `public/audio/promo-beat.wav`; `scripts/prepare.mjs` does both when their sources change. `src/promo/PromoSound.tsx` lays them with WEATHER ALERT's jingle and the outro's.
- STORMTRACKER's globe needs WebGL 2: `remotion.config.ts` and `scripts/stills.mjs` render with Chrome's GPU through ANGLE, and `scripts/prepare.mjs` copies its pictures of the earth from `design/textures` into `public/textures`, which is not committed.
