# Backdrop

The studio's backdrop: a faint ring at the top right of the studio gradient, and the signal it sends out.

- With `animate` on, the ring sends out its signal: a wave every 3 s that leaves the ring and spreads across the studio to two and a half times its size, fading as it goes, 9 s from the ring to gone. Each wave is placed from the clock as it is drawn, so every backdrop sends the same waves at the same moment, and a picture drawn again later shows them where they would have been.
- Without `animate`, as in a still frame, the ring stands alone, and so it does with reduced motion.
- It sits behind everything on the 960 × 540 stage, laid out from the ring's centre at (830, 170). Mount it first on a `Stage`, or first in a layer of its own, so everything else is drawn over it.

`Broadcast` stands the board, THE HOUR IN REVIEW and an outro's board underneath on it. A picture that stands on the studio's ground draws the backdrop whole, the ring and its signal together.
