# TopBand

The channel's mark (`Mark` at 40px): KLWD in chrome, and nothing else.

- Optional: `mark` (default KLWD).
- It sits at the title-safe inset, x 40, level with the listing. On hover, the close button covers its first letters. Its right end stays empty for return.
- The clock is not here: it sits at the bottom right, in the ticker. Nor is a LIVE pill: LIVE is the word on a working session's pill, and one LIVE on the stage is enough.

Show it on every frame, the stinger included. Never put news in the top band: it is the channel, not the story.
