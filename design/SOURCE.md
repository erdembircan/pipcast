# The design system

This folder began as a copy of the PiP Newsroom design system, which defines everything KLWD looks and sounds like: the graphics, the motion, the jingles' scores, and the rules for what airs when. The artifact is the design's prototype; this folder is what the product draws with, and it changes here when the product needs it to.

- Source: https://claude.ai/artifact/GnLta8btVSfqWWe83uPvcx
- Copied from version 48 (`1792599111-0a56`) on 21 October 2026
- Changed here since:
  - The board's ring (22 October 2026). It sends out its signal, a wave every 3 s that keeps time with the clock, as chosen on PiP Newsroom · Board motion (https://claude.ai/artifact/3cbyt3B4yT8ttGrM6tCiVk), and an outro's board now shows the ring as it fades in. The artifact keeps the still ring.
  - Stacking, in Event desks (22 October 2026). Only what lands while you are away from Claude airs: the stories waiting when you switch to Claude are dropped, and so is a story that lands while you are there. The report on the hour still waits. The artifact still has stories wait in the ticker while the window is hidden.
  - The Ticker's crawl (22 October 2026). It moves on a belt at a steady 53 px a second instead of a CSS animation, keeps each item's words until it has gone past the tab, and carries on across cuts. The artifact keeps its old crawl.
  - The Ticker's belt (23 October 2026). It is a layer of its own (`will-change: transform`), so the crawl moves it each frame and redraws nothing, and the window takes about a quarter less of the Mac's processor while it shows. The artifact has no belt.
  - Data sources (23 October 2026). Lines changed are counted in every working tree a session's work runs in, a subagent's worktree among them, after each call that can change files, so they reach the hour they are written in and a commit keeps them. The artifact still counts them with git as each turn starts and ends.
  - The chrome words (24 October 2026). BREAKING NEWS and the hour's words draw their outline on a layer of its own under the chrome, so it runs round the outside of each letter. Stroked on the letters themselves, it also traced where the font's pieces overlap, and each R showed a dark box where its bowl meets its leg. The artifact still strokes the letters.
  - On the hour, in Event desks and Open questions (24 October 2026). A report joins the queue only if the window shows as the hour strikes, so a window hidden at the hour skips that hour's report; a report already waiting keeps its place while the window is hidden, until the next one comes due. The artifact still airs a missed report when the window shows within ten minutes of the hour.
  - Background commands, in Data sources (24 October 2026). A dev server, a watcher or a log a session leaves running in the background no longer keeps it working: its turn's story airs as the turn ends. The artifact still counts every background task as work.
  - More than four (24 October 2026). KLWD takes every session tuned in, as chosen on PiP Newsroom · More than four (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jgfrd35WD6unpDQM6v2BoR). The listing shows four rows a page and pushes to the next page every 8 s, with a tab under the pills saying which page; THE HOUR IN REVIEW pages the same way, 10 s a page; and the band is one plate, KLWD and TUNE IN or TUNE OUT, with no count. The artifact still has four sessions at most, the chip with its four cells and the tab beside it.
  - Project plates (24 October 2026). Each row of the listing leads with its session's project on a plate, as chosen on PiP Newsroom · Project in the listing (https://claude.ai/artifact/NXxiDEvgaGRnffdbLXeRUs): the strap's dark ground under a gold rule, leaning forward like every tab, the name in caps, 200 wide so the titles start in line, and a longer name cut with an ellipsis. The artifact's listing names no project.
  - Claude's full-screen Space, in Frame (24 October 2026). The window is on every Space but the ones Claude fills alone in full screen, and stays hidden while you are on one, so it never shows over Claude as you switch to it; and it hides at once, with no fade. The artifact's Frame still puts the window on every Space.
  - Motion on the compositor (24 October 2026). The stinger and the ident, intros and outros, hand each run to the compositor as keyframes drawn from the same pictures, instead of drawing the page again from a timer 30 times a second: in Low Power Mode that timer ran late and the page got 30 frames a second at most, so they stuttered while everything else stayed smooth. To move by transform and opacity alone, the glint crosses the chrome words as a window sliding over a bright copy of each word, the words' shadow sits on their outline and chrome, the hour's rays turn as one piece, and the lower third's glint rides a sweep as wide as the headline. Clawd is a layer of his own, so his steps no longer redraw the key light and the city behind him. The artifact still draws its opens from a timer.
  - Transparent window, in Frame (24 October 2026). A setting that fades the whole window to 40% (`pip-faded`) while the pointer is elsewhere, so what is under it shows through, and makes it solid again, with the controls, while the pointer is on it. The artifact's window is always solid.
  - Usage limits (24 October 2026). The board shows the account's usage limits under the listing, as chosen on PiP Newsroom · Usage limits (https://claude.ai/artifact/6iHFA7BcvtkCfBryG9BPJt): SESSION and WEEK in the ticker's gold caps, each on a meter filled to the share used, with its figure, gold from 70% and red from 90%. The artifact's board shows no limits.
  - Session in the review (24 October 2026). Each row of THE HOUR IN REVIEW reads as the session's row on the board, as chosen on PiP Newsroom · Session in the review (https://claude.ai/artifact/NS4MXPHooWtFDVoybJEjYf): its project on the listing's plate, then its title over the hour's figure, so sessions that share a project can be told apart. The artifact's review names each row by its project alone.
  - The Transparent window button, in Frame (24 October 2026). Transparent window has a hover button of its own, as designed on PiP Newsroom · Transparency button (https://claude.ai/artifact/HsE5nQDm91CUFJ3cWzXuph): a fourth glass circle at the top right, 8 pt to the left of sound, showing a window with solid contents while the setting is off and the same window with its contents a checkerboard while it is on. A press turns the setting over. The artifact's window has three controls.
- The designs: PiP Newsroom · Station (https://claude.ai/artifact/LaUs1pnGjjsvs9W2PfR6hS), PiP Newsroom · Window (https://claude.ai/artifact/D4c6cARgruAfEgBvdoUt8Q) and PiP Newsroom · Band (https://claude.ai/artifact/LrMxrQF2mTCNXeLssgf9j1)

The broadcast draws with `components/bundle.js` and `components/bundle.css` as they are. Make the product's changes here and list them above. Copying the artifact's files over this folder again would undo them, so bring anything new from the artifact across by hand.

`components/Band`, `components/PromptSketch`, `components/Pip`, `components/PipControls` and `components/PlayWithSound` are review-board pieces. The product draws the band in the mod and the window natively, so they are here as the reference those follow, not as code that runs.
