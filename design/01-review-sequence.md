The station is designed on the station canvas, one page per part of the broadcast, in the order the feed airs. Each stage decides one thing, and every stage after it is built on that decision.

## How a review works

- Comment on a frame on the station canvas, or on this system.
- When a stage is approved, its row here changes to **Decided** and the decision goes in the last column. The next stage opens then.
- Every frame is the same components fed different scenes, so a change in this system reaches every page of the canvas at once.

## Decided ahead of its stage

- **The mark (stage 2):** KLWD in chrome, alone. Chosen on the Brand page over the gold swoosh, a rule, speed stripes, a badge and a chevron. The bug, the opens and the hour's open carry no hook, and the mug's tab lost its swoosh.
- **One open for every story (stage 5):** BREAKING NEWS carries every story the engine reports: needs your input, finished (READY FOR REVIEW or WORK COMPLETE) and stopped on an error. JUST IN is gone. Stories air first come, first served: nothing cuts in, a story that lands during another waits as COMING UP, and one that is cut off (you answered it while it was on air) is not restarted. There are no roundups: TOP STORIES is gone.
- **The open's background (stage 5):** the glass wall, a wall of glass panes seen at an angle on the station's navy, lit by a red wave. It replaced the rays and globe behind the opens, which were too busy.
- **Intro, desk, outro (stage 5):** every BREAKING NEWS segment closes with an outro, the intro run out, and its own closing jingle.
- **No title on the open (stage 5):** it ends on its words; the gold strip with the show and the session is gone. The session is first named at the desk, on the strap.
- **Desks run 10 s (stages 5 and 6):** every story's desk, the hour's headlines and THE HOUR IN REVIEW.
- **The station clock (stage 6):** the dial, picked on the KLWD station clock canvas over the ticker's clock stepped up, chrome figures and the wall monitor: the monitor's chrome bezel round a glossy blue face, gold hour marks, chrome hands and a gold seconds hand that steps with the pips. The hour's open has no strip or subtitle: ON THE HOUR stands alone beside the clock.
- **No sessions (stage 4):** no window. With nothing tracked, no PiP is shown.
- **No limit (stages 4 and 8):** the station tracks every session tuned in. The listing shows four a page and pushes to the next every 8 s, with a tab saying which page; THE HOUR IN REVIEW pages the same way, 10 s a page; the band is one plate with no count, and TUNE IN never dims. Chosen on PiP Newsroom · More than four (https://claude.ai/artifact/Jgfrd35WD6unpDQM6v2BoR).
- **Sound (stage 7):** on and off from the window's own hover control; it starts on and the window remembers the choice.
- **Every hour the same (stage 6):** no report is special, 18:00 included.
