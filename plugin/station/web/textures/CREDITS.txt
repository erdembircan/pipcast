earth.jpg, topo.jpg, water.jpg and clouds.jpg are NASA's Blue Marble pictures of the Earth: its surface, its relief, where its water is, and its clouds. NASA's imagery is in the public domain (NASA Visible Earth, visibleearth.nasa.gov).

They come from the example images of the three-globe npm package (MIT License, Copyright (c) 2019 Vasco Asturiano). clouds.jpg is that package's cloud picture's transparency, saved as a greyscale JPEG.
